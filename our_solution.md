## What is **[NAME FIX THIS BEFORE SUBMISSION]**?

**[NAME FIX THIS BEFORE SUBMISSION]** is a solution for mounting handheld laser scanners to collaborative robotic arms and automating their operation through our advanced software. This allows existing scanning hardware to be reused, reducing costs and improving sustainability. It also enables human-robot collaboration, with the technician being able to easily remove the scanner from the robot and performing manual scanning tasks such as those that still require physical manipulation of the object. The collaborative robot is controlled using industry standard protocols, making it robust and adaptable to a variety of robot solutions. In the same manner, our software is built to be adaptable to a variety of different scanners.
