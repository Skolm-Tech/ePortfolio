# Laser Surface Scanning with Robotic Automation

## Our Problem

Laser surface scanning is a process that uses laser light to capture the exact shape and details of a physical object, to a very high degree of precision. 

Currently in industry, this is done via a tedious manual process, with an operator holding the scanner at various angles to the object, until the scan is completed. 

The Einscan projects a pattern onto the object and uses cameras to triangulate the 3D position of surface points, which requires the scanner to be held at a specific, optimal distance and orientation relative to the surface for accurate data capture.

This process is very limited, particularly by the human operator, as the variety in scanning angles, distance and methodology that are inherent to human movement can limit the precision and accuracy of the scan. 

Humans cannot replicate an exact scan path twice, which means that scans are not easily repeatable, and scan quality is heavily limited by the skill of the operator. The automation of quality inspection is a key challenge in the move in industry towards "smart manufacturing".

This project aims to reduce these errors and the overall efficiency of the scan by replacing the human element with a robotic arm, and fully automating the process, solving one of the key obstacles towards "Industry 4.0".
