# Laser Surface Scanning with Robotic Automation

## Our Problem

Laser surface scanning is a process that uses laser light to capture the exact shape and details of a physical object, to a very high degree of precision. Currently in industry, this is done via a tedious manual process, with an operator holding the scanner at various angles to the object, until the scan is completed. This process is very limited, particularly by the human operator, as the variety in scanning angles, distance and methodology that are inherent to human movement can limit the precision and accuracy of the scan. This project aims to reduce these errors and the overall efficiency of the scan by replacing the human element with a robotic arm.
